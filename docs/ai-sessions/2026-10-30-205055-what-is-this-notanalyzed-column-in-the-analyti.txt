● What it is: not_analyzed_transfers counts the sales in that year with sale
  code AA, "SALE NOT ANALYZED". The assessor's office never reviewed those
  sales, so none of them could get the "love and affection" code. That means any
  family transfers among them are missing from family_transfers.

  Why it's in the table: in most years the count is tiny, but in a few years
  it's large enough to make the family numbers too low:

  ┌────────────┬───────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────┐

  │            │ year)                 │ understated                       │
  ├────────────┼───────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────┤
  │ 2018 /     │ ~2,000 / ~1,700       │ somewhat understated              │
  │ 2019       │ (5–6%)                │                                   │
  ├────────────┼───────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────┤
  │ 2020       │ under 10 a year       │ negligible                        │
  │ onward     │                       │                                   │
